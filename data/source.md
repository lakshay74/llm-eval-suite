# CampusLib Library FAQ

The library is open from 8 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday. It is closed on Sunday.
Students may borrow up to 5 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
The late fee is Rs 2 per day for each overdue book.
A book can be renewed online up to twice, if no one else has reserved it.
For a lost book, you must pay the replacement cost plus a Rs 100 processing fee.
Study rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours at the front desk or in the library app.
Printing costs Rs 1 per page.
Guests can use the Wi-Fi after showing a valid ID at the front desk.
